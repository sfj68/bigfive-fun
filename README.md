# bigfive-fun

Live at **https://sfj68.github.io/bigfive-fun/**

A client-side Big Five personality test. Answer 120 real IPIP-NEO items and get your actual
trait scores, plus four different matches to consider: a book character, a movie character,
a TV character, and a real historical Iowa State University figure, each picked by how close
their profile is to yours.

The question bank and scoring rules are ported from [bigfive-web](https://github.com/rubynor/bigfive-web)
(MIT licensed — see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`). The character roster, matching, and result
presentation are original to this project. The ISU figures are real people; their entries are
individually fact-checked and written as factual statements about documented achievements,
not invented personality judgments.
